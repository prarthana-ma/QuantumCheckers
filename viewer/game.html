Quantum Checkers

A playable quantum-combinatorial board game, with the research spine attached.

Start

Checkers where a piece can be uncertain — spread across several squares with a chance of being found on each. Resolve it by choice, or let a capture force it. Five stages teach one mechanic at a time, and every one of them is explained the first time you reach it.

Stages unlock as you complete the one before. Each stage teaches one mechanic.
